USEFUL INFORMATION

Plan Your Journey Better

Keep these important travel considerations in mind.

01 / BEFORE BOOKING

Travel Planning

Decide your preferred travel dates, group size and accommodation preferences before making your enquiry.

02 / DOCUMENTS

Travel Documents

Check the documents and identification required for your journey. Confirm the latest requirements before departure.

03 / CONFIRMATION

Booking Details

Keep your booking confirmation and travel arrangements accessible throughout your journey.

04 / PACKING

What to Pack

Prepare suitable clothing, personal essentials and any items you may need during your stay.

05 / TRANSPORT

Travel Arrangements

Review your confirmed departure, arrival and transfer arrangements before starting your journey.

06 / PERSONAL PREPARATION

Personal Essentials

Carry your personal essentials and any necessary medication. Prepare according to your individual travel needs.
